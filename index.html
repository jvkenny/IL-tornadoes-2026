Illinois had a tornado year
unlike any on its record.

Between 1 January and 13 August 2026 the National Weather Service logged preliminary tornado reports in Illinois — the state's own average for those dates, and the highest count in the twenty-three years the reports have been archived. Four plates on what that year looked like, and what the number actually means.

Reports are not tornadoes

The 283 is a count of reports, and it should never be printed as a tornado count. One tornado crossing four counties files four reports. Where 2026's damage surveys are already complete, Illinois has 68 confirmed tornadoes against 143 reports — a ratio of , of the fifteen states with enough reports to measure one, and well below Illinois' own historical .
